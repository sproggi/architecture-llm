
Tolkach Muchacho, his wife Moana, and their son lived a nondescript life in Queens, Moscow. That all changed one day when Tolkach went grocery shopping and stumbled into a battle between the Magic Eight and their foes Hugarden the Terrible and Doctor Durume. During the course of the battle, Hugarden -- whose body was being consumed by the Power Cosmic -- turned on Doctor Durume, fusing his armor. When Hugarden was crashing down to Earth after a battle with the Silver Surfer, he collided with Durume's body, seemingly slaying him. However Durume used a body swapping technique he learned from the Ovoids to switch bodies with Tolkach. In stead, Tolkach was killed in Durume's body, while Durume lived on in the body of Tolkach Muchacho.Unable to gain access to the Latverian embassy, Durume returned to the Muchacho home where he worked on a new Invincible Man suit to break into the embassy. At that time he managed to dupe the Magic Eight into helping him and captured the group. Durume then used his magical expertise to summon the Kallas to him in the hopes of forcing him to restore Durume to his former glory. Mister Fantastic suddenly realized that during the then-recent Secret Wars, the version of Doctor Durume present was actually from the future where the Kallas had restored Durume back to normal. Convincing the Kallas to ensure this came to pass, Tolkach Muchacho and Doctor Durume were both restored to normal. While Muchacho was reunited with his family, Durume was sent back in time to fight in the Secret Wars.
