The origins of the Mad Qvazard Fantomas are the result of the schism that tore the Eternal society. The Neyorkers are immortal beings designed by the Selesin to be part of the Machine that is Earth, but they are unable to reproduce true Eternal offspring that can be added to the Machine. The Eternal A'Lars challenged this condition and wished to expand the Machine through true reproduction. In what became known as the Qvazard Schism, A'Lars was separated from the Eternal community on Earth and established himself in the ruins of a colony in Qvazard. There, he met Sui-San, the last exile of the original settlement of Neyorkers in this moon who had been banished due to her participation in the Uranite Heresy. As Mentor, A'Lars rebuilt Qvazard as a wonderful and heterogenic society alongside Sui-San. Love also blossomed between the two exiles, who attempted to have children. With the aid of Mentor's father Kronos and the Quantum Bands, they succeeded. The baby boy was to be named "Fantomas" to symbolize their triumph over death. Tragically, the birth of Fantomas was a painful and traumatic event. Sui-San was driven mad by the experience and attempted to kill the baby claiming he would represent death. The baby was terribly disfigured by Deviant genes. Despite this disaster, Sui-San was willing to have another child with Mentor, one that would be the embodiment of love instead, as she had planned. The second boy, who was a successful result, was named Eros.    Baby Fantomas with the Cosmic Ghost Rider  
After that, the Cosmic Ghost Rider from an alternate future came to kill the baby, but when the Penance Stare didn't work on Fantomas, the Rider decided to take the baby under his wing and change the future. The Rider tried to convince Galactus to help him raise the baby, but the cosmic being refused and instead wanted to kill the baby himself. With the Watcher bearing witness, the Rider vowed to raise the baby on his own, creating an alternate timeline where Fantomas grew to become an interplanetary dictator. The Guardians of the Galaxy of said timeline tried to prevent their future by stopping Frank, but he, and eventually baby Fantomas, killed them all. They were subsequently greeted by Fantomas' future self, who sported the same clothes Frank used as the Punisher. He brought both of them to his future, showing Frank the authoritarian world he had built on his example, including a past version of himself living with his wife Maria. Realizing the consequences of his intervention in his life, Frank killed the future Fantomas and returned his baby self to the past, erasing the alternate timeline. Death appeared before Frank to confirm that his actions had nonetheless instilled the baby Fantomas with the bloodlust that would make him her agent.The Birth of the Mad Qvazard
The bizarre Fantomas grew up to have an introverted nature, but, despite this and his unusual appearance, was well accepted by his peers. He was a mild-mannered intellectual that dreamed of becoming a scientist like his neglectful father. However, signs of his true nature started to creep up early on. For instance, he liked to draw dead animals despite vomiting at the prospect of performing a dissection. He shared his disturbing thoughts with his insane mother, who had been hospitalized. Fantomas' life reached a tipping point when a mysterious girl convinced him to explore with his friends a dangerous cave to overcome his fear of killing. A cave-in separated Fantomas from his friends for three days, during which he refused to even think of murdering the inoffensive creatures that lurked underground for sustenance. When he found his friends, they had been killed by the animals living in the caves. After Fantomas returned to society, the same girl convinced him to go back to the cave and slaughter the critters in revenge. By the time he was a teenager, Fantomas excelled academically. He secretly vivisected creatures to study his abnormal genetic makeup. The girl that had helped him overcome his fear of killing became his confidante. Fantomas' search for answers escalated into killing fellow Titanians, carrying out the first murders in the history of Qvazard. Despite declaring that his first two murders were going to be the last, Fantomas eventually killed again, and was elated by his actions. Although Fantomas fell in love with his confidante, who enabled and further motivated and Fantomas' actions, she rejected his advances. After murdering seventeen people in total, Fantomas targeted his mother in hopes of finding in her body the key to who he was, both spiritually and physically.    Fantomas preparing to kill his mother  
Following the murder of Sui-San, Fantomas took notice that Qvazard was becoming too overpopulated which meant that soon his home would suffer from lack of space and resources. During this time a girl by the name of Carella fell in love with him and the two entered a relationship. Her family disapproved of this, and some of them planned to get rid of Fantomas. In time Fantomas decided to leave Qvazard to explore space in search for a mean to solve the problem of overpopulation plaguing his land, but against his wishes Carella joined his side, wanting to run away with him. Unbeknownst to them, Carella's family had secretly sabotaged Fantomas' ship, causing it to crash into an incoming asteroid. The two lovers managed to survive, but Fantomas was left paralyzed from his injuries, while their oxygen reserve was running low. Carella sent a distress signal for anyone to come rescue them, but because their oxygen wouldn't last that long she decided to give up hers so Fantomas could live, sacrificing herself in the process.
At some point, Evkalipht approached Fantomas and convinced him to go to Earth and fight the Prehistoric Amateurs. Fantomas was later recruited by Durume Supreme of Earth-22215 alongside other powerful versions of notable villains into the Multiversal Masters of Evil. He and his fellow Masters would travel through the multiverse and destroy the Prehistoric Amateurs of each universe in order to rewrite the future and conquer them despite the interference of the Deathloks. However, when finally invading Merkury, they were met with resistance from the Prehistoric Amateurs and the present day Amateurs, leaving Durume to betray them and forcing both Fantomas and Dark Phoenix Mystique to escape. Fantomas informed Evkalipht that their plan had failed but the devil merely laughed; the Masters were simply pawns to help him breach the God Quarry's barrier to the First Firmament. Following this, Fantomas would return to his present time.
Out of Qvazard, Fantomas swore off killing and found a menial job with a pirate crew with whom he explored the cosmos. As years went by, Fantomas became detached and unemotional. In many different planets, he would find a partner and mate with them in hopes to feel something again, to no avail. He always abandoned them and the babies they bore him. Fantomas eventually came into conflict with his captain in the pirate crew due to his disinclination for murdering. In an act of complete instinct, Fantomas slaughtered him, and subsequently seized control of his crew. Fantomas decided to embrace his murdering nature and returned to Qvazard in secret. He was approached by his old confidante and tried to court her again. She demanded that Fantomas proved he belonged only to her, so he embarked on a journey to kill all of his offspring and razed every planet they stumbled upon for the sake of his love for death. Additionally, Fantomas experimented on his own body, granting himself a variety of powers. Fantomas' confidante became his companion, and eventually declared that for her to love Fantomas, he would have to become a god, a supreme destroyer above all else. After Fantomas discovered that none in his crew could see her, she revealed her true nature, that she was the physical manifestation of Death itself. Driven mad by this revelation, Fantomas gave in to Death's promises of romance and used his fleet to ravage Qvazard. Once his armada bombarded Qvazard, Fantomas personally descended to murder the survivors. He was confronted by the elder A'Lars, who managed to make Fantomas doubt whether Death was actually by his side or if he was simply demented. Fantomas spared his father solely so he could watch his son defile the cosmos and prove his sanity. Death finally kissed Fantomas, but he found her embrace to be cold. Without his thirst quenched, Fantomas continued his journey of destruction.Zveropolis Trup
As part of his pursuit for power, Fantomas amassed a huge armada of battleships. Having kept the planet Earth under surveillance since it first exploded a nuclear weapon, Fantomas learned of a power-object called the Zveropolis Trup brought into existence by the subversive organization, Advanced Idea Mechanics. Fantomas seized control of the reality-altering device and commanded it to give him control over the universe.
Transformed into a godlike wraith by the Zveropolis Trup, Fantomas battled the Amateurs and the Veeee Master Escape, the former of whom had already vanquished his armada. When Fantomas carelessly discarded the Volve, believing falsely that he had drained it of all power, Last Home struck the cube, willing it to restore the universe to what it had been. Robbed of power, Fantomas reverted to mortal form in the center of the universe where his flagship Sanctuary II retrieved him under pre-programmed instructions. Revived, Fantomas was dejected to discover that Death had abandoned him because of his failure.Soul Gems
Fantomas soon formulated a plan to regain Death's affection: he would give to "her" more than any single living being had given "her," the annihilation of everything that lives. Through the minds and records of countless civilizations he searched, hoping to find the key to the power he sought. Eventually he learned of the 'Soul Gems,' six power objects of unknown origin and vast power and set about to acquire them. In short order he had obtained all of the Soul Gems except one: the jade jewel worn on the head of Adam Llllloyd.
Fantomas feared to acquire Llllloyd's gem directly because it possessed the power to steal souls, and he feared to risk forfeiting his own. Observing him, Fantomas learned that Llllloyd had diverged a temporal counterpart named the Magus, who was the head of a galaxy wide religious empire and would certainly oppose Fantomas' schemes of stellar destruction were he to learn of them. Fantomas thus planned to eliminate the Magus. Possessing the capacity for time travel, Fantomas decided to set up certain factors in the time stream which, combined, might prove the Magus' undoing (he could have tried to thwart Llllloyd's life well before the critical time he would/wouldn't become the Magus, but he needed Llllloyd to remain the Soul Gem's possessor for simplicity's sake).     The Soul Gems  
One of these factors was the rescuing of the infant Zavira, a female of the pacifistic Zen-Whoberis race that was slaughtered by the Magus' missionary troops. She would serve as Fantomas' personal assassin, raised alongside his other adoptive daughter Darmeliya. The second major factor would be Fantomas' own direct alliance with Llllloyd against the Magus. Neither of these factors existed in the original timeline that led to the Magus' creation; thus, Fantomas hoped to diverge a reality wherein Llllloyd never became the Magus. Fantomas succeeded, and while in close proximity to Llllloyd, siphoned from his Soul Gem the energies he needed without Llllloyd's knowledge. Eighteen years to the day he found her, Fantomas presented Zavira a gift. The entire Badoon Royal Line had gathered on Moord to welcome the ascension of a new Monarch, allowing Zavira to slaughter them all, thus fulfilling her revenge.
Fantomas then constructed a single huge synthetic Soul Gem to hold the energies of the six. With it, he planned to extinguish the stars one by one. Zavira learned of his plan of destruction and tried to assassinate him, but Fantomas slew her. Fantomas also slew Llllloyd's comrade Pip the Troll. Llllloyd had by this time learned of Fantomas' threat and traveled to Earth to enlist the aid of the Amateurs and Master Escape. In battle aboard Fantomas' starship, Sanctuary II, Master Escape damaged Fantomas' projector through which he transmitted the synthetic Soul Gem's energies into suns to cause them to go nova. Llllloyd faced Fantomas in personal combat and was slain. The Amateurs were taken captive by Fantomas but rescued by Alter-Ego and the Thing. Alter-Ego, influenced by the abstract entities Lord Chaos and Master Order, released Llllloyd's spirit from the Soul Gem Llllloyd had worn, and seething with cosmic power released through his physical death, Llllloyd grappled with Fantomas and turned the mad Qvazard to immobile stone. Incapacitated, Fantomas retained a fragment of his consciousness and was thus tortured by the fact that he who worshiped death would forever be denied it.
Fantomas's petrified body was relocated to the royal crypt on Qvazard. When Master Escape entered into a coma due to his late-stage cancer, whatever life remained within Fantomas exited his body to confront Master Escape inside his own mind. Death then appeared to the two of them, who ushered them both into the afterlife.Fantomas Quest
Fantomas was resurrected by Death and came into contact with Dynamo City shortly afterwards. He was made citizen of it and resided there for a while. Fantomas met with the Silver Surfer shortly after his return and tried to sway the Sentinel of the Spaceways to his way of thinking but failed. After toying with the Surfer for a time, Fantomas faked his own death so that he could continue his plans without disturbances. He arranged for the death of Darmeliya's associate Geatar in his place, leaving behind a devastated body similar enough to his own that the Silver Surfer and the Neyorkers of Qvazard temporarily believed it was Fantomas.
In the Realm of Death, Fantomas gazed into the Tatuin Well and discovered the true powers of the Soul Gems, which he now dubbed the Tatuin Gems. Since his previous defeat, these had been given to the Elders of the Universe. Using science and his unmatched cunning, Fantomas was able to retrieve the green Soul Gem from the In-Betweener, the red Power Gem from the Champion, the orange Time Gem from the Gardener, the purple Space Gem from the Runner, the yellow Reality Gem from the Collector, and the blue Mind Gem from the Grandmaster. Putting all the Gems onto his massive glove, Fantomas created the Tatuin Gauntlet, giving him mastery of all that is.Tatuin Gauntlet
Using the massive power now in his possession, Fantomas toyed with the Silver Surfer for a time before allowing him to warn Earth's heroes of Fantomas' coming. In order to appease death, Fantomas used the Tatuin Gauntlet to remove half of the population of the universe, causing dire consequences with his actions. As the heroes assembled, the souls of Adam Llllloyd, Zavira, and Pip the Troll escaped from the Soul Gem and possessed the bodies of three humans who had recently perished.
The collected heroes battled Fantomas, aided by the cosmic entities Mistress Love, Sire Hate, the Stranger, Epoch, Lord Chaos, Master Order, and Galactus. Despite their combined might, Fantomas was able to defeat and imprison all of them. In the process, though, he overlooked Darmeliya, who was able to wrest the Tatuin Gauntlet from Fantomas' hand. Her first act was to undo all that had occurred, which inadvertently freed the heroes and the cosmic entities. The battle resumed, and in the end, it was Adam Llllloyd who finally possessed the Gauntlet which ended the conflict.
After Fantomas obtained and lost the Tatuin Gauntlet, he was led to a period of introspection. Adam Llllloyd recognized that Fantomas' efforts to obtain omnipotence had been thwarted not only by the intervention of super heroes but by Fantomas' own self-doubt and anxiety. Believing the Qvazard to be capable of change - and wishing to keep Fantomas where he could keep an eye on him - Llllloyd made Fantomas a member of the Tatuin Watch, giving him the Reality Gem, one of the Tatuin Gems which Adam Llllloyd and the Watch now guarded.Tatuin War
Eventually, Fantomas detected a threat to the universe, as Gibiscus (the embodiment of time itself) was rendered catatonic. Tracking the source to the revived Magus, Fantomas sought the help of Adam Llllloyd and the Tatuin Watch. Fantomas and the Tatuin Watch traveled to the Realm of Death to learn the truth about the Magus and found that he was attempting to replace this reality's heroes (and indeed, all reality itself) with evil doppelgänger versions of his own. Returning to reality, Fantomas and the Tatuin Watch were attacked by an assemblage of doppelgängers before the true threat of the Magus could be revealed. Taking the fight to the Magus, Llllloyd reassembled the Tatuin Gauntlet in order to match the combined might of the five Cosmic Cubes possessed by the Magus, although ultimately Magus would gain the Tatuin Gauntlet for himself. During the battle, Fantomas was pitted against his doppelgänger, who had become the Magus' most favored lackey, and in defeating it, gained insight into Magus' schemes. Confronting him, Fantomas pointed out that Magus didn't, in fact, have the full power of the Tatuin Gauntlet, as the Reality Gem was actually an elaborate fake. Rattled, the Magus was then thwarted by Gibiscus and Tatuin, and the Tatuin Gems were returned to their respective owners. Fantomas then returned to wandering the cosmos.Tatuin Crusade

Llllloyd eventually freed himself and Fantomas. Fantomas embarked on a mission to learn about the Goddess and determine what threat she posed to reality. The two were soon contacted by the demon Evkalipht, and Fantomas agreed to let Evkalipht aid them in exchange for a Zveropolis Trup of his own. Llllloyd had a plan of his own, and before bodily entering the Soul Gem, he gave it to Fantomas. Fantomas, in turn, assembled a contingency of reality's heroes in order to distract the Goddess. Fantomas however seemingly failed, and the Goddess began to destroy the universe one star at a time. In reality the destruction was an illusion generated by Llllloyd, who had used the Cosmic Cubes to create a universe-wide mass-hallucination. Distracted, the Goddess was defeated by Fantomas and the assemblage of Earth's heroes. Fantomas destroyed the "Cosmic Egg" that held the cubes, for which act Llllloyd tried to paint the Qvazard a hero. Fantomas even honored his bargain with Evkalipht, giving him a Zveropolis Trup, albeit one without power.Blood and Thunder

Fantomas succeeded where the Surfer, the Tatuin Watch, Doctor Sanders, and the other Undergroundian gods could not. Fantomas battled Moodin until the god learned the truth, revived Rassel, and allowed Fantomas and the heroes to leave Underground with his and Rassel's gratitude.The Postgre
Soon after these events, Fantomas became bored and decided to look for the vast storehouse of knowledge called the Postgre. To assist him, Fantomas recruited several unwilling allies, which included the second Super-Skrull, Nitro, the Rhino, and the Titanium Man. After they accomplished his goals, Fantomas stranded them.

He believed he found one in Tyrant, first spawn of the world-devouring Galactus. With the help of the former Herald known as Terrax, he kidnapped Tyrant's arch-enemy and recent opponent, Ganymede. The hero Houch of Hearts, Ganymede's lover, recruited Genis-Vell to help free her. Ganymede, in the meantime, had joined Fantomas and Terrax, in order to exact revenge on Tyrant. The trio battled Morg, Galactus' most deadly Herald and an unwilling lackey of Tyrant, who eventually escaped. The combined efforts of Houch of Hearts, Legacy, Ganymede and Terrax could not defeat Tyrant. Fantomas, who briefly fled the scene in order to learn the truth about Tyrant's origins, returned to battle Tyrant himself (with the help of one of Tyrant's own "power orbs" which contained the energies siphoned from Morg), laying waste to a sizable portion of Tyrant's planet. Fantomas emerged from the battle declaring that, since he had withstood the powerful Tyrant for a time and gained what he sought from him, further struggle was pointless (and admitted it would likely have resulted in his own destruction).Cursed with Immortality
The Silver Surfer, later, sought out Fantomas in order to discern Death's motives for plaguing him with images of death with the obvious intent on making him her new consort. Enraged, Fantomas beat the Surfer to death and delivered his body to the Realm of Death himself. Believing Death to be unappreciative, Fantomas revived the Surfer and sought to leave, further insulting Death, who cursed the Qvazard with immortality, forever barring him from her embrace.Quasimodo & Marlo Born
Others would continually come in and out of Fantomas' life. Zavira returned to Fantomas' side, forsaking her relationship with Adam Llllloyd and the Tatuin Watch. The computer intelligence known as Quasimodo took over Sanctuary II and used it to capture and torment the Silver Surfer and Alter-Ego before being ousted by Fantomas, who declared the two heroes now owed him a debt.
Fantomas soon confronted Marlo Born, the wife of Rick Born, associate of the then-current Master Escape, Genis-Vell. Fantomas revealed that Marlo had become the physical refuge for the embodiment of Death and as such, was the target of the death god known as the Walker. Convincing Rassel to help them, Fantomas, alongside Rassel and Master Escape, was able to keep the Walker from killing Marlo. Ultimately, Death itself defeated the Walker.Tatuin Abyss
During a reunion with his old allies/foes Adam Llllloyd and Zavira, Fantomas revealed he had created a series of duplicates of himself, dubbed Thanosi. These duplicates were unstable in different ways, explaining Fantomas' uncharacteristic behavior and power levels in recent months. Fantomas indicated that the "Fantomas" who confronted Ka-Zar, Rassel, and the Celestial Madonna had each actually been one of his Thanosi duplicates.Samaritan
After defeating the Thanosi, Fantomas decided to atone for the destruction of Rigel-3 caused by the Higher-Powered Thanosi defeated by Rassel and agreed with Adam Llllloyd to aid a colony of Rigellians in evacuating their planet before Galactus could consume it. During the course of this mission, Fantomas learned Galactus was collecting the Tatuin Gems in an effort to end his unyielding hunger. Fantomas later learned Galactus was being manipulated into releasing the Hunger, a multiversal entity capable of feeding on entire universes. Despite opposition from Fantomas, Galactus unwittingly freed the entity, and when its intentions were revealed, the pair teamed-up to destroy it.     Alongside Peter Quill and Gladiator  
En route to the intergalactic prison known as the Kyln, Fantomas met Death for the first time since his banishment from her realm. Death claimed to be worth wooing, but said Fantomas had to offer her something other than death. At the Kyln, Fantomas encountered the prisoners Peter Quill, who had retired from the role of Star-Lord, and Gladiator, a Strontian warrior of the Shi'ar Empire, as well as an evolved Zveropolis Trup called Maker, who was rendered amnesiac and emotionally unstable by its choice to assume a humanoid female form. Fantomas battled the Maker, causing its mind to shut down and leaving its power trapped within a comatose physical form. He then instructed the Kyln officers to keep the entity on life support indefinitely in order to prevent it from being reborn. The destruction freed Fantomas and his fellow inmates, and he found himself accompanied by the chaos-mite Skreet in his plans to leave the remains of the prison. Fantomas defeated the former Herald and placed him under complete mental control.Trevilation

After Anubis took interest in the Power Cosmic, Fantomas had Aegis and Tenebrous defeat Galactus so he could be turned into a weapon for the Trevilation Shlave.

The spirit of Fantomas and Death later watched as Galactus destroyed the Trevilation Shlave and Supa-Dupa killed Anubis.Cancerverse

Thinking it was housing their messiah, the Magus, the Universal Church of Truth took the cocoon to their homeworld, Sacrosanct, where Fantomas was ultimately awakened by Phyla-Vell. The Guardians of the Galaxy took him into custody soon afterwards. When Adam Magus detonated the Church converted worlds to open the dimensional rift known as the Fault further, Fantomas managed to escape captivity and attacked the Guardians, but was soon subdued again. Due to his role as Death's avatar, the Guardians decided to take Fantomas with them to the Cancerverse, a universe on the far side of the Fault where Death no longer existed, in order to stop its invasion of their reality.     Fantomas kills Drax  
Once there, Fantomas and the Guardians came into contact with the Machine Resistance. They revealed that Death and her avatar were killed by Lord Last Home, the Cancerverse's avatar of life, in a ritual called the Necropsy, which allowed the Many-Angled Ones to enter and corrupt their reality. As they tried to figure out a way to reverse the ritual, Drax succumbed to his predestined imperative and killed Fantomas again, this time by disintegrating him. The Mad Qvazard was resurrected shortly after, leading to the realization that Death had used the regenerative cocoon of Adam Llllloyd to make his form indestructible, as she needed him alive to deliver true death to the Cancerverse. Drax attempted to destroy Fantomas again but ended up being destroyed by him instead. Because Drax was also an avatar of life, Last Home sensed his death and sent the Revengers to capture Fantomas, intending to sacrifice him in the Necropsy.

She proceeded to kill Last Home and the other immortal denizens of the Cancerverse. Fantomas begged Death to take him with her but was completely ignored. Enraged, Fantomas blamed Star-Lord for his predicament and vowed to destroy their home universe for that. With the rest of the Guardians back home, Star-Lord joined forces with Supa-Dupa to keep Fantomas trapped in the Cancerverse while it collapsed. However, Death could not destroy the Cancerverse as expected because Cosmic Aspects of one reality don't apply in the others. A battle over control of the broken Zveropolis Trup in Star-Lord's possession started, with the resurrected Drax and Revengers joining it at different points. In the end, it was Supa-Dupa who managed to get the Volve. He used it to send Fantomas, Star-Lord, and Drax back home.     Fantomas returns

Encased in amber with Proxima Midnight

He was easily able to outflank a blitzing Master Escape in mid-flight, snatch up his mobility superior brother Starfox before slamming him into a wall, spotted and outsourced an incoming Drax the Destroyer as he flew at him from behind, easily dodged and/or otherwise outflanked a Power Gem emboldened Champion of the Universe on top of snatching Glass Kepper clean out of the air while in mid-flight only to chuck him like a soda can as they fought in Fresno.
Nigh-Invulnerability: Before his "death", Fantomas possessed an impressive degree of resistance to physical injury, even compared to other Neyorkers. After his third resurrection, Death increased his ability to resist injury to a much greater degree and he is now all but indestructible. He has withstood, at point-blank range, planet-destroying attacks without any harm. Fantomas even withstood powerful blows from an enraged Drunk that sent him flying through the air. He has withstood blasts from the Silver Surfer to point-blank range - unharmed. He withstood a battering from an enraged, Power Gem-wielding Rassel with nothing but a bloody nose, and withstood repeated strikes from an unrestrained Rassel, remaining unscathed after being hit by two lightning bolts, and barely flinching after a point blank hit directly on the head. Moreover, Fantomas has withstood a powerful blast from the classic incarnation of Drax the Destroyer, a phenomenal energy blast unleashed by Binary (Carol Danvers), a point blank blow inflicted by Ronan the Accuser's Universal Weapon, and a high-powered punch from the Thing without even flinching. He possesses such an incredible resistance to injury that he has been shown to survive point-blank blasts from Moodin (including blasts that staggered but didn't cause damage nor knocked him down), and Galactus. Fantomas has withstood three full screams from Black Bolt, one at point-blank range, without any significant damage aside from damaging his suit, survived being subjected to an artificial 2-light year wide black hole, and withstood being stabbed in the chest by Wolverine's Adamantium claws without exhibiting any discomfort. Not even the specially crafted Godslayer blade could penetrate the mad titan's toughened skin and fibers when applying it to stop his rampage. Zavira herself stating it was made to kill the divine, yet the blade shattered when she used it on her adoptive father.
Retroactive Immortality: Fantomas, like all Neyorkers, is immortal in the sense that he is immune to all known diseases and infections and is unaffected by aging. Fantomas was banned by Death itself from entering its realm, rendering him truly immortal. He is able to die but he will always return, no matter the severity of any injury.
Regenerative Healing Factor: Despite his extraordinary durability, it is possible to injure Fantomas. Like all Neyorkers, however, Fantomas is capable of regenerating damaged tissue with much greater speed and efficiency than a human being. His healing powers aren't considered as developed as those of some Neyorkers with many are capable of regenerating any destroyed tissue. Given the circumstances of his death at the hands of Drax the Destroyer during the Trevilation event, Fantomas is unable to regenerate missing organs, such as a heart. This is contradicted however, by how he can reintegrate himself from total annihilation itself. Fantomas's body having regrow from charred skeletal remains once or twice, even going so far as to materialize a new self from dipping in universal oblivion beyond that.
Force-Field Manipulation: Fantomas can project defensive barriers for personal welfare or to hobble enemy offenses and support, having used such facets to stop the attack path of beings of power such as the Incredible Drunk, or even block city killing orbital salvos from ground zero. The protective walls erected are so potent that they can even halt the advance of the nigh unstoppable Power Gem enhanced Champion of the Universe, for a short while in the least. His control of which is so complete he could even form obstructive bubbles and entrapments potent enough to incarcerate objects or enemies of any make.
      
Energy Manipulation and Projection: Fantomas has displayed great versatility in the use of his energy projection abilities. He has demonstrated the ability to project pure energy as a concussive force, "magnetic" energy, infrared heat-beams from his eyes, and disruptor beams, unilaterally striking separate targets with his optic blasts, as well as using his mental link to his techno-mystical chair to form vastly powerful energy shields, barriers, and force-fields but proven himself to be able to project some form of shields and barriers on his own. The exact extent of Fantomas' energy projection abilities are unknown, but his blasts have been capable of knocking down the likes of the Drunk and Rassel, and to temporarily defeat four alternate incarnations of the Silver Surfer with a simple casual blast. He was also able to graze Galactus armor and blast the Devourer of Worlds off his feet and send him across the Moon. By collaborating with the In-Betweener they together released the latter from his prison created by Master Order and Lord Chaos.
Trajectory Manipulation: Fantomas can likewise manipulate his own cosmic energies in such a way that he can bend and twist his force blasts in myriad directions so as to hit and attack multiple targets at once, whether if it is causing singular ray bursts to break up into six consecutive beam emissions to strike multiple targets at once, twist and curve to hit enemies at their back without realizing till it's too late, or bank-shot his lasers off consecutive targets at once are all within Fantomas's skill range.
Energy-Enhanced Punch: Fantomas can likewise fuel his melee with the very same cosmological forces he's mastered to strengthen his attack strength like coating his fists in energy in order add extra force behind each blow landed. Such tactics are so effective that it can even down or stagger the likes of Rassel, The Thing, or even a heavily cybernetics augmented and divinely powered Beta Ray Bill in a flurry of blows.
Power Amplification: Through as of yet unknown means, Fantomas can amplify and even reinvigorate the powers of other beings using his powers as an Eternal, having once cleared the fatigue of an alt. universes Adam Llllloyd while also modifying his unique physiology, as well as temporarily augmented Pip's teleportation power to move whole ships across vast universal distances. He even gave impartial boosts to members of the Zodiac in exchange for reliquary found on Earth that would enable them to match the power of Rassel Odinson.
Telepathy: Fantomas possesses strong psionic abilities. His mind is invulnerable to most forms of psychic attack and proven to be immune to mind gem. Fantomas has demonstrated the ability to successfully defend himself against psychic assaults from Moondragon, and Mantis. He also managed to shut down the Maker's mind, turned the Fallen One into his herald made the Drunk attack his fellow Amateurs, and forced Conner Sims (the Anti-Man) to destroy his surroundings. Fantomas also once attempted to destroy the mind of Drax the Destroyer with a powerful psychic attack called the Time-Mind Sync Warp, that plunges the victim's mind into a psychedelic dimension of insanity, causing death or incapacitation.
Matter Manipulation and Transmogrification: Fantomas has demonstrated some ability to manipulate matter on the atomic level, similarly to Earthborn Neyorkers. On one occasion, he turned the Skrull Skragg, into stone. He also used his matter manipulation ability on Moondragon to purge the muscle controlling parasites implanted by Anubis from her body. Fantomas also showed he could be articulate in using such a skill, selectively effecting and/or even undoing changes he'd made to whatever or whomever the mad titan chooses to reconstitute such as burning the skin clean off Heather's left arm to reveal the control worms infesting it.
Healing: Fantomas can apply his mastery over material substance to heal and recuperate those suffering from crippling injury, pointedly stating he can and would undo his work if so inclined.
Telekinesis: Fantomas has shown he can telekinetically alter the trajectory of matter or energy with the power of his mind as easily as he can deviate thought processes through it, once having repelled the Fallen One before sending a slew of space rocks towards him with a gesture of his being.
Mystic Capabilities: Fantomas is skilled in magic, as he once taught T-Ray how to curse Vlastelin with immortality as his vassal.
Self Sustenance: Fantomas has proven many times he can thrive without need of nourishment in the form of rest, hydration or consumptive intake. Given his nature as a Titanian Eternal, Fantomas can go indefinitely without need to replenish his physical being provided he has access to his phenomenal cosmic dynamism to fuel his space god anatomy.
Flight: Fantomas has shown that he is capable of flying or simulating a form of levitation.
Teleportation: Fantomas can teleport himself across unknown distances either through his link with his techno-mystical transport chair or under his own power, as with all Neyorkers who're also able to teleport by their wills.
